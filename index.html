About
Gustav Bam Founder, Bam Cloud AI · Azure Solutions Architect Expert · MCT

25 Years of Enterprise IT. Now Building Smarter Clouds.

Based in Durbanville, Cape Town, I've spent over 25 years as a Senior Infrastructure Engineer and Microsoft-certified Azure Solutions Architect — designing, building, and running enterprise infrastructure across South Africa, with a particular depth in financial-sector environments.

My most defining engagement was leading the IT consolidation of multiple Sanlam Group subsidiaries into a single, centralised Group IT model — spanning Active Directory, Exchange, SQL Server, and Windows Server migrations at scale, using Quest Migration Manager. That project shaped how I think about complexity, governance, and getting things done right the first time.

Bam Cloud AI brings that same rigour to Azure architecture, cloud migrations, and IT training — whether you need a trusted architect for a complex project, a coach for certification prep, or someone who can teach your team to work confidently in the cloud.

The honest reason I do this: I like watching the moment someone stops guessing and starts knowing. I have spent four decades on a French horn learning that mastery is built through unglamorous, disciplined repetition, not shortcuts — and that is exactly the standard I hold training to. If a session does not leave you able to do the thing yourself, without me in the room, I have not done my job.
