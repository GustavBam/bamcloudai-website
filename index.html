About

25 Years of Enterprise IT. Now Building Smarter Clouds.

I'm Gustav Bam — a Senior Infrastructure Engineer and Microsoft-certified Azure Solutions Architect based in Durbanville, Cape Town. I've spent over 25 years designing, building, and running enterprise infrastructure across South Africa, with a particular depth in financial-sector environments.

My most defining engagement was leading the IT consolidation of multiple Sanlam Group subsidiaries into a single, centralised Group IT model — spanning Active Directory, Exchange, SQL Server, and Windows Server migrations at scale, using Quest Migration Manager. That project shaped how I think about complexity, governance, and getting things done right the first time.

Bam Cloud AI brings that same rigour to Azure architecture, cloud migrations, and IT training — whether you need a trusted architect for a complex project, a coach for certification prep, or someone who can teach your team to work confidently in the cloud.
